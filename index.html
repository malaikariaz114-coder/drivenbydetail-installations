FAQ

Good questions.

Can't see yours? Call 01323 692026 or send us a WhatsApp.

Will a hardwired dash cam drain my battery?

No. We set up parking mode with battery protection, so the camera switches off before your battery gets too low to start the car.

Will I see any wires?

No. Cables are routed behind the trim and headlining for a clean, factory-style finish.

Can you add a reversing camera to my car's original screen?

Often, yes. Depending on your vehicle we use an input adaptor or a vehicle-specific interface. We'll check compatibility before quoting your final price.

How long does an installation take?

Most single installs are done the same day. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm your booking.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Mobile fitting is available up to 20 road miles from North Harbour, as long as there's a suitable workspace and the weather is dry. Combined jobs pay just one mobile fee.

Can you fit equipment I've already bought?

Yes. Fitting only starts from £89 for a front dash camera, £159 for rear parking sensors and £169 for a head unit. You supply a complete, compatible kit and we check it first.

Is the price on the form final?

It's an estimate. We confirm your final price after checking your vehicle, and always before any work starts. No VAT is charged.
